## Explicit non-goals

- No timer schedule, day-of-week scheduling or time-window entities.
- No generic MIoT write endpoint.
- No *routine* Xiaomi Cloud calls during device operation. One-time user-consented browser OAuth2 login is implemented in onboarding; it remains isolated from the BLE control path.
- No pairing, factory reset, OTA or firmware modification.
